This year at the ENRICH Robotics Hackathon, we explored a previously inaccessible section of the nuclear power plant in Zwentendorf, Austria.
The main objective was to localize and map multiple radioactive sources under conditions of varying difficulty.
Challenges included poor or no illumination, weak or absent Wi-Fi connectivity, and the need for cooperative mapping using multiple robots.

A key novelty of this year’s challenge was the deployment of a heterogeneous team of robots, combining ground and aerial platforms to enhance coverage.

We successfully mapped the accessible ground area with our DRZ Telemax and the newly deployed EC Swift. Beyond mapping radiation sources, we also generated a colored 3D point cloud by fusing LiDAR and camera data.

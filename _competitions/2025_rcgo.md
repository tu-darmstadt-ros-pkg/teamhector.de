At the RoboCup German Open 2025 in Nuremberg, our team competed for the first time with our newly developed rescue robot Athena.

Despite completing the final assembly on site, we successfully conducted initial field tests in the competition arenas and even managed to score a few points. These trials provided valuable insights ahead of the World Championship later this year.

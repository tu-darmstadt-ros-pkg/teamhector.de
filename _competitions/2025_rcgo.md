At the RoboCup German Open 2025 in Nuremberg our team competed for the first time with our recently self-developed robot Athena.

Despite having to complete the final assembly on site we able to field test Athena in the Arenas and score a few points. This allowed us to discover many important areas of improvement for the world championchip later this year.

At the RoboCup German Open 2024 in Kassel, our team proudly secured 1st place overall, along with awards for Best in Class Dexterity and Best in Class Autonomy.

This year, a new chain design was used for the first time, which reduces weight and friction compared to its predecessor. This was achieved using specially manufactured chain links from the 3D printer.

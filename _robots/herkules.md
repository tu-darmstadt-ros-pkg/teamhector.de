Our robot Herkules is a Telemax Evo Plus by Telerob used for research of autonomous assistance functions for urban search and rescue robots as part of the [Kiara Pro](https://rettungsrobotik.de/kiarapro/) project.

The Telemax Evo Plus is an improved version of the Telemax Evo Hybrid platform we previously used particularly in terms of stability and carry strength. 

The base platform is equipped with rubber tracks and four flippers which can modify the track geometry in order to overcome obstacles.

In a compact configuration, the robot has the dimensions of 0.86 m x 0.68 m x 0.73 m and weighs 112 kg. In close range it can lift up to 80 kg. Fully stretched out, the arm can reach more than 1.5 m and still can carry 15 kg.
Standing on its flippers the robot can reach a height of 2.5 m. Thereby, enabling versatile manipulation capabilities.

We are currently developing a modular autonomy box including a continuously rotating lidar to create 3D point clouds, an omnidirectional camera and a RGBD-camera to enhance sensor coverage and aid our research goals.

Our robot Herkules is a Telemax Evo Plus by Telerob, used for research on autonomous assistance functions for urban search and rescue robots as part of the [Kiara Pro](https://rettungsrobotik.de/kiarapro/) project.

The Telemax Evo Plus is, for us,  a more suitable version of the [Telemax platform](https://www.teamhector.de//robot/drz-telemax/) we previously used, particularly because of its increased stability and carrying capacity.

The base platform is equipped with rubber tracks and four flippers, which can modify the track geometry to overcome obstacles.

In a compact configuration, the robot measures 0.86 m x 0.68 m x 0.73 m and weighs 113 kg. In close range, it can lift up to 80 kg. Fully stretched out, the arm can reach more than 1.5 m and still can carry 15 kg.
Standing on its flippers, the robot can reach a height of 2.5 m. This enables versatile manipulation capabilities.

We are currently developing a modular autonomy box that includes a continuously rotating lidar to create colored 3D point clouds, omnidirectional cameras, and RGBD cameras to enhance sensor coverage and support our research goals.
